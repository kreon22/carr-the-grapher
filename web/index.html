Carr the Grapher

Go out alone into country nobody has drawn. Listen, row, sound the depths, and come home with a map. Then hand it to the people who rule, and find out what they make of it.

Best with sound. Your atlas and the world's memory of your maps are kept between visits.

The Council House, Corvalle

A commission from the Crown

By order of the Crown of Aster.

To Carr, licensed grapher of Corvalle: you are commissioned to survey Lake Kalvas on the eastern frontier. Its shores, its depths, and where its waters go. Deliver a fair copy to the Council on your return. The fee will be paid according to the map's use.

Chancellor Ivo Brandt,
for His Majesty Aldren IV
